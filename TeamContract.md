# Team Contract


---
## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---
## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

* We will use Discord to communicate with each other.

* We will use a priority to designate response times:
  * Low Priority: 1 Day
  * Medium Priority: Within the same day
  * High Priority: 4 Hours

* High Priority messages should be sent to the #high-priority channel, low and medium priority message should be sent to the general channel.

* Meet in Discord call as necessary. 
* 
* Things should a teammate notify you about:
  * Missing a deadline (high priority)
  * A lecture or lab that you are not attending (low priority)
  * Questions about code (low priority)
  * Anything else (medium priority)

* Respectful and inclusive behaviour are necessary for smooth and productive communication. What are some respectful and inclusive behaviours you expect when communicating with each other during lectures, labs, or outside of class? (Examples: Actively listening to each team members ideas, giving everyone a chance to meaningfully contribute, etc.)
1. Feedback should be constructive and actionable, not just a comment.

## Decision Making

* How will decisions about your team project be made? (Examples: By majority vote, by unanimous vote, etc.)
* Decision making will be made through Consensus
* If no agreement is possible, then a majority vote will be required.

---
## Conflict resolution

* How will your team resolve conflicts? (Example: by listening to each other's side of the issue and attempting to reach a compromise. By consulting a TA or instructor as a team, if a resolution has not yet been reached.)
* For disgareements on coding practices, disputes will be resolved through discussion and code reviews
---

## Accountability

* Reliability and accountability are also important aspects of teamwork. What are the responsibilities of each team member? (Example: completing their share of the work in a timely manner, seeking assistance from teammates/TAs/instructors when required, etc.)
* Completing work in a timely manner
* Keeping the rest of the team up to date on any changes to the project, issues, or deadlines.
* Seeking help where needed.
* 100% of the work will be completed by the end of the course.
---

## AI

* AI should not be used to complete entire segments of the project.
* AI may be used to write unit tests, and to assist in the learning of core concepts
* AI may be used in part for visual design, in particular for polish.
* Code written with AI assistance should be understood fully by the student, and should be commented to explain the AI's decisions.
